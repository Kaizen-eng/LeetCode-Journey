## 𝐈𝐧𝐭𝐮𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧:

✦ 1000 is the first number that contains a comma.
✦ Every time we multiply the threshold by 1000, we reach the next comma position:
1000 → 10⁶ → 10⁹ → 10¹² → 10¹⁵
✦ For a threshold t, every number in [t, n] has a comma at that position.
✦ Therefore, its contribution is simply:
n - t + 1
✦ Since n ≤ 10¹⁵, there are only a handful of thresholds to process.
